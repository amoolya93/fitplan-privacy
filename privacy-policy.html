Privacy Policy for FitPlan

Last Updated: May 20, 2026

Introduction

FitPlan ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in our fitness and meal planning application.

Information Collection and Storage

Local Storage Only

FitPlan stores ALL data locally on your device. We do NOT:

  • Collect or transmit personal information to external servers
  • Track your activity or behavior
  • Share your data with third parties
  • Require account creation or login
  • Use cloud storage or sync services

Data Stored Locally:

  • Family member profiles (name, age, weight, height, fitness goals)
  • Meal plans and custom recipes
  • Workout adherence records
  • Instagram handles you've saved
  • Anthropic API key (if you provide one)
  • App preferences and settings

Third-Party Services

Optional Services:

1. Anthropic API (Optional)

  • If you provide your own API key for AI recipe search
  • Recipe queries are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service
  • Subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy

2. Instagram Integration (Optional)

  • Opens Instagram links in your browser
  • No data shared with Instagram beyond standard web browsing

3. Grocery Shopping Services (Optional)

  • Deep links to Swiggy (India) or Instacart (US)
  • Ingredient lists copied to your clipboard
  • Subject to those services' privacy policies

4. Expo Development Platform:

  • App built using Expo/React Native
  • No analytics or tracking enabled
  • No crash reporting configured

Permissions

FitPlan requests the following device permissions:

  • Storage: To save your data locally on your device
  • Notifications (Optional): To send meal plan and progress reminders

Data Security

  • All data remains on your device
  • No network transmission of personal data
  • No server-side storage
  • Your data is as secure as your device

Data Deletion

To delete your data:

  1. Use the "Reset All Data" option in the Profile tab, or
  2. Uninstall the app

This will permanently delete all locally stored information.

Children's Privacy

FitPlan does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is designed for family use with parental supervision for minors.

Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be posted within the app and on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • All data stays on your device
  • You can view, modify, or delete data at any time
  • You can export data by backing up your device
  • You can delete all data via the app's reset function
